01 · What it does

Answers from your knowledge base

02 · How it works

RAG: retrieve first, then answer

The bot doesn't "memorize" your documents or fine-tune on them. On every question it first retrieves the most relevant chunks from your knowledge base and answers strictly from what was found. Update a document — the answer updates immediately.

01

Documents

Your files: help center, policies, PDFs, website pages, past support tickets.

02

Index

Split by headings into sections and indexed for search. Re-indexes automatically when docs change.

03

Retrieval

Finds the most relevant chunks for the query. Nothing above threshold → honest "I don't know".

04

Answer + citations

The model answers strictly from retrieved chunks and cites sources.

grounded

Anti-hallucination is a mechanism, not a promise. When no chunk passes the relevance threshold, the LLM isn't even called — the bot immediately returns an abstention message. There is nothing to invent from.

04 · AI inside

RAG is retrieval + language model

The bot first retrieves relevant sections from your knowledge base, then a Large Language Model (LLM) formulates the answer and cites the source. The live demo in the hero shows the retrieval layer running in your browser with zero token cost. In production, the final reply is phrased by your chosen LLM — swappable via config.

Frontier cloud models

Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) — top reasoning quality, always up to date, connected via API.

Regional & compliant APIs

Any OpenAI-compatible provider in your jurisdiction so billing and data residency match your compliance rules.

Self-hosted / on-prem

Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral on your own server (vLLM / Ollama) — data never leaves your perimeter, zero per-token fees.

Tailored to your trade-off

We help pick the right balance of quality vs cost vs privacy. You can launch on a cloud API and switch to self-hosted later.

Honest note about the on-page demo. To keep the browser demo instant, free, and key-less, it runs the retrieval layer with extractive snippets. Production answer fluency comes from pairing that retrieval with an LLM on your documents.
