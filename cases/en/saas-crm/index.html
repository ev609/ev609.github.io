01 · What it is

A vertical CRM for service businesses, running in production

Full-cycle product

Not a landing page or prototype: self-serve onboarding, subscription tiers, billing, isolated tenant data, and automated messaging.

Real users & payments

The platform runs 24/7 with live accounts and billing. It also powers the public commercial web presence of a service-center network.

Vertical focus

02 · Engineering

What this proves for your project

  • FastAPI + PostgreSQL backend — REST API, multi-method auth (password, OTP code, magic link), and background workers.
  • React / TypeScript frontend — marketing pages, tenant dashboards, lazy-loaded bundles, and host-based branding.
  • Strict multi-tenancy — every tenant's rows and credentials are isolated by tenant_id.
  • Billing & plan quotas — subscription state machine, quota enforcement, payment webhooks, and invoicing.
  • Integrations — WhatsApp Business, Telegram, MAX, per-tenant SMTP, and payment gateways.
  • Production operations — zero-downtime deploys, health checks, backups, and ongoing maintenance.

Honest disclosure. Ladero is our own in-house product rather than a client commission. We showcase it as proof of engineering maturity: we design, ship, and operate multi-tenant SaaS systems over the long haul.
