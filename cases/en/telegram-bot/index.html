01 · What it does

Handles first contact for you

Button-driven menu

Customers never get lost: services, pricing, lead form, or free-text question — all one tap away. No "message a manager and wait".

Step-by-step lead capture

The bot asks for name, contact, and task description, shows a summary for confirmation, and immediately forwards the structured lead to your manager in Telegram.

Knowledge-base answers

Common questions (timelines, payment terms, warranty) are answered instantly. If a question isn't covered, it honestly says so and offers to leave a request.

Nights, weekends, or traffic spikes — the bot responds immediately. You receive qualified leads with contact details instead of missed messages.

03 · Architecture

Clean architecture — logic decoupled from Telegram

The bot's state machine is separated from the network transport. That makes it testable offline without a BotFather token, while Telegram plugs in as a thin adapter layer. That's what separates a maintainable turnkey bot from a fragile script.

04 · AI inside

Deterministic menu. AI for free-form questions

Structured flows (menus, buttons, multi-step lead capture) run on a deterministic state machine without an LLM — fast, predictable, and impossible to derail. AI is plugged in where it adds value: understanding free-form questions and answering from your documents via RAG.

Frontier cloud models

Regional & compliant APIs

Any OpenAI-compatible provider so billing and data residency match your compliance rules.

Self-hosted / on-prem

Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek on your own server (vLLM / Ollama) — data never leaves your infrastructure.

No vendor lock-in

Switch models via configuration. Knowledge retrieval runs on our dedicated RAG engine (see related case study).
