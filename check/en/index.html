    04 · FAQ

    Common questions

    How does this page know what IP ChatGPT and Claude see?

    ChatGPT, Claude, and most AI platforms sit behind Cloudflare, which exposes a CORS-enabled /cdn-cgi/trace endpoint on each domain. Your browser queries it directly along your real route — so even split-tunneling configurations show the exact exit IP and country each service sees.

    Direct route shows a different country than ChatGPT — is that a leak?

    No, that is standard split-tunneling: local traffic goes direct while AI domains exit via VPN. As long as all OpenAI/Anthropic subdomains share a supported country and WebRTC/DNS are clean, split-tunneling is healthy.

    Why do different OpenAI or Claude domains sometimes show different IPs?

    Some VPN setups load-balance domains across multiple nodes. If chatgpt.com and api.openai.com exit from different IPs or countries, anti-fraud may see concurrent logins from separate locations. Route all domains of one provider through a single exit server.

    Does AlfaFond log or store my IP?

    No. This page is pure static HTML/JS hosted on GitHub Pages with no backend or database. Recent check history is stored strictly in your own browser’s localStorage.
